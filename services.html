How we help

Therapy for the mind and the body.

Therapy means coming alongside you to understand what’s holding you back. We help reconnect the parts of you that got stuck in the past or tangled up in worry about the future, so you can live here, now.

Where we focus

Trauma, anxiety and depression.

Trauma therapy

Trauma can be one big event, like an accident or a loss. It can also build up quietly over years: emotional neglect, growing up around addiction or mental illness, never feeling seen.

Talking about it is often not enough. We use body-based, “bottom-up” methods like EMDR and somatic work to help your nervous system feel safe again.

Anxiety

Worry, what-ifs, rumination and panic can take over your days. Fighting anxiety with more thinking often makes it louder.

The goal isn’t to never feel anxious again. It’s to manage anxiety so it stops managing you.

Depression

Hopeless, isolated, not yourself. Maybe you feel like a burden, or like nothing is going to change.

“When you can’t look at the bright side, I will sit with you in the dark.”
Approaches

Proven methods, matched to you.

No single approach fits everyone. Diane draws on several, depending on what you need.

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing helps painful memories lose their charge, without reliving them in detail. Many people find it far more manageable than they expected, even empowering.

Somatic Experiencing

Trauma lives in the body as well as the mind. Somatic work helps you notice and release stress that talk alone can’t reach.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

A practical, solutions-focused approach for spotting the thoughts that pull you toward hopelessness, and changing how you respond to them.

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy

Life is hard, and some thoughts and feelings will show up no matter what. ACT helps you get unhooked from them so you can move toward the life you want.

Attachment work

For the deep longing that can come from not feeling understood as a child. The brain can build new pathways that help you comfort, and even re-parent, the part of you that still hurts.

Self-compassion & mindfulness

Learning to meet your own mistakes and struggles with kindness instead of criticism. Research shows it’s one of the strongest sources of resilience we have.

Narrative & strength-based therapy

Re-telling your story with your strengths in view, not only your struggles.

Hypnosis

A natural state of focused, relaxed attention that can support change when used alongside other therapy.

With Scott Clark, CPC

Life coaching

Coaching is for when you’re ready to look forward: clarifying what you want, setting realistic goals, finding what’s in the way, and building a plan to get there.

A coach doesn’t tell you what to do. Scott partners with you so you can reach your full personal, relational and professional potential. He works with people 16 and up, in person or by telehealth.

  • Major life transitions
  • Career change and retirement
  • Relationship shifts or endings
  • Leadership development
  • Life purpose

Coaching isn’t covered by insurance. Your first consultation is free.
