Find us & get started

Taking the first step is the hardest part.

You don’t need to know exactly what to say. Reach out however feels easiest and we’ll take it from there.

1 · Reach out

Call or email

785-333-3793

diane@ethostherapyandlifecoaching.com

Please don’t include private health details by email. A sentence about what you’re looking for is plenty.

2 · A quick conversation

We’ll find a fit

We’ll call you back to hear a little about what’s going on, answer questions, check insurance and find a time that works.

3 · Your first session

Paperwork, then you

Before your first visit we’ll send intake forms through our secure client portal. Then come in, or log on, and we’ll get started.
