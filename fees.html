Insurance

We accept most major plans.

  • Blue Cross Blue Shield
  • Tricare
  • Aetna
  • UnitedHealthcare Community
  • Trustmark
  • Meritain
  • Private pay

Your plan may cover therapy in full or in part. After you schedule, we’ll ask for a copy of both sides of your insurance card, and our biller can help answer coverage questions.

Questions to ask your insurance

  • Does my plan include mental health benefits?
  • Do I have a deductible? Have I met it yet?
  • Is there a limit on sessions per year?
  • Do I need approval from my primary care doctor?

Payment

We accept cash, check, HSA cards and all major credit cards. Current clients can also pay through the TherapyNotes client portal.

Frequently asked

Thinking about therapy for the first time?

I’m used to handling things on my own. Isn’t therapy for people who can’t?

Not at all. Knowing when you need help, and reaching out, takes courage. Everyone needs support sometimes. We’ll help you find your strengths and use them to make your problems smaller.

Why not just talk to a friend or family member?

Friends care, but a therapist has the training to help you see your situation differently, learn new skills, and listen without judgment or expectations. Therapy is also completely confidential. And sometimes leaning too hard on a friend during a painful time makes it hard to be around them once you feel better.

Shouldn’t I just take medication?

Medication can help, and sometimes it’s the right call alongside therapy. On its own it can’t solve everything. Therapy works on the patterns and problems underneath, and builds on your strengths.

What happens in a session?

It depends on you and your goals. We tailor every session to what you need, whether that’s talking, learning skills, EMDR or body-based work.

How long will therapy take?

Everyone is different. It depends on what brought you in, your goals and your commitment. We’ll check in on progress together as we go.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Both Diane and Scott see clients in person in Suite 207 or by secure video from home.

How do I get the most out of therapy?

Show up, be honest, and practice between sessions. Your active participation is the biggest factor in how much it helps.

Still have questions?
