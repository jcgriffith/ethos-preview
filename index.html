Meet Diane

Diane Clark, LSCSW, CCTP

Diane has spent her career helping people restore peace, hope and self-compassion. Clients describe her as warm, direct and easy to talk to. Most feel at ease within the first session.

Finding us

Upstairs in the Marshall Building.

We’re in a historic building on 4th Street in downtown Manhattan. First visits can feel like a lot, so here’s exactly where to go.

  1. Find the Marshall Building

    121 S. 4th St. Look for the “Marshall Building” lettering above the glass doors.

  2. Head up to the second floor

    Ethos is listed on the building sign as Suite 207. Take the stairs up and follow the hallway.

  3. Look for the “E” on the door

    Suite 207 has a yellow door frame and our logo on the glass. Come on in.
